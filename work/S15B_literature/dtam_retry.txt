2. Method

The overall structure of our algorithm is straightforward.
Given a dense model of the scene, we use dense whole im-
age alignment against that model to track camera motion
at frame-rate. And tightly interleaved with this, given im-
ages from tracked camera poses, we update and expand the
model by building and refining dense textured depth maps.
Once bootstrapped, the system is fully self-supporting and
no feature-based skeleton or tracking is required.               Figure 1. A keyframe r consists of a reference image Ir with pose
