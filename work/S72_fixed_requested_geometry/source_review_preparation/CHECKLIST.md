# S72 source/protocol audit preparation

This is preparation for a later exact-source review, not approval of unpublished code or an observed geometry result. Fixed scope: real history 19 against all real targets 20–23, using saved S69 requested cameras and the existing S71 matching convention. No arrays, images, GT pose body, models, or weights were read or executed here.

1. **Select the authoritative camera fields by ID.** S69 `execution_01/optical_cameras.json` describes `optical_cameras.npz` SHA `599543102f5c799ca4005ead413b69ee10e42269ef36a7942410f9066556d325`: `ids:int64[9]`, `c2ws:float64[9,4,4]`, `rgb_timestamps_ns:int64[9]`. Read the saved IDs instead of assuming positions. These are RGB-time TUM optical camera-to-world matrices, translations in meters, x right/y down/z forward. IDs 19/20 reuse S52; 21–23 use the already-accepted S69 interpolation. No new interpolation or GT reading is needed. Preserve the interpolation and approximate-calibration qualifications.

2. **Use optical relative pose in the correct direction.** Write source and target c2w as Cs=[Rs,ts], Ct=[Rt,tt]. Source-to-target coordinates obey R=RtᵀRs and t=Rtᵀ(ts−tt). Set E=[t]×R and F=Kt⁻ᵀ E Ks⁻¹, so the constraint is x_targetᵀ F x_source=0. Save R,t,E,F, camera IDs, K identities and baseline norm for independent arithmetic. Do not apply the VMem local-basis flip again: S69 `consumer_raw=optical_FP32@diag(1,-1,-1,1)` is a different representation, and get_cond subsequently restores the optical basis. The preferred FP64 optical archive needs neither this conversion nor arm centering/scale. A common rigid world-coordinate change and common positive translation scaling preserve the epipolar constraint up to F scale; mixing camera representations or arm scales does not.

3. **Bind K to the actual image coordinate system.** `geometry.json` pins geometry NPZ SHA `ade8813c6c0b63f3570edf3452fa1d4d04ab62ebe8072640e54092e68b889ff7`; its `ordered_ids:int64[8]` and `K_pixels_576:float32[8,3,3]` are sufficient camera metadata fields. Do not decode its latent/conditioning fields merely to obtain K. History order is [19,18,13,12], then targets [20,21,22,23]; verify the actual ID field on execution. S69 used five identical S68 crop Ks and copied history 19's K for targets. S68 original helper scales pixel K rows by resized width/640 and height/480, then subtracts crop left/top from its principal point: for this declared path, 640×480 → 768×576 → center crop 576×576, left 96/top 0. Use saved K values, not a new calibration or a guessed focal length. `K_normalized_576` and 72² latent-ray coordinates are not interchangeable with native 576² SIFT coordinates.

4. **State pixel-center semantics explicitly.** S71 saves OpenCV `kp.pt` as (x,y), in its native image coordinates, without transposition or a half-pixel addition. VMem's separate dense-ray grid uses i+0.5; that is not a reason to add 0.5 only to the saved SIFT coordinates. If a common pixel-coordinate translation A is deliberately used, both points and K must transform (x′=Ax, K′=AK); equivalently F′=At⁻ᵀ F As⁻¹. The inherited scaled/cropped default ROS K and non-undistorted images are approximate. Hash agreement does not establish physical lens calibration or exact resampling-center optics.

5. **Retain a fixed, unbiased matching scope.** Reuse S71 native RGB→gray, fixed SIFT settings, two-direction L2 knn2, strict ratio<0.75 and mutual identity, with all four pairs retained. History 19 is real input, not an A0/B frame or a target-to-generated comparison. Bind its preprocessing and PNG identity to the same crop contract as target references. Save all accepted keypoint IDs/xy before testing the fixed requested F, plus finite/degenerate masks and every residual. Do not estimate/retune F, select matches using that F before reporting all-match error, or quietly reuse the unrelated S71 homography consensus. A known F can be evaluated with fewer than eight matches; eight is not a prerequisite for fixed-camera residual arithmetic. Zero matches remains explicit insufficiency, and few/concentrated matches remain weak evidence.

6. **Specify residual and degeneracy handling before values.** For q=x_targetᵀ F x_source, Sampson squared distance is q² divided by the sum of the first-two-component squared norms of F x_source and Fᵀ x_target. State whether the report uses its square root (approximate pixel distance) or the squared quantity (pixel²); do not call raw q a pixel error. Record nonfinite/near-zero denominators rather than replace them by a value that makes errors look small. Record the meter baseline before any E/F normalization: zero baseline/pure rotation gives E=0 and cannot pass by producing zero numerator; a predefined tiny-baseline numerical guard is an insufficiency, not evidence of calibration accuracy. Points near epipoles, repeated textures, moving objects, occlusion, small support and false matches limit interpretation. Fix runtime limits/create-only output/failure retention and save all four pair outcomes; do not introduce a residual success cutoff after seeing results.

A later finite source review should check this actual wiring and the fixed readlist. Residual agreement can support consistency of these selected real-image correspondences with the declared requested-camera convention; it cannot prove correspondences true, identify generated camera poses, establish complete calibration, or assign S70 framing mismatch to memory selection. No generation, new model arm, warped score or new mechanism is implied.
