# Conditional camera response and correspondence selection

Status: source-based question and ordinary baseline; no method selected or validated. This note does not change the frozen S76 experiment or its metric. No S75/S76 scientific results were read.

The precise question is whether an apparent improvement in response to the requested camera change survives accounting for which correspondences disappear. Appearance changes can remove difficult matches without improving camera obedience. A low residual on the survivors therefore does not alone identify improved response over the original observable population.

| Primary neighbor | Actual mechanism and boundary |
|---|---|
| [Shah et al., Selective Regression Under Fairness Criteria, ICML 2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/shah22a.html), §§2.2–4.2 | Defines coverage and risk conditional on acceptance. Its examples show that aggregate improvement as coverage falls can coexist with worsening subgroup risk. Sufficient representation conditions involve conditional independence or matching conditional means and variances; a confidence score alone does not establish them. Applying this warning to image regions or geometric difficulty is our analogy, not the paper's camera claim. |
| [Lindenberger, Sarlin and Pollefeys, LightGlue, ICCV 2023](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Lindenberger_LightGlue_Local_Feature_Matching_at_Light_Speed_ICCV_2023_paper.html), official [source at eb42fee2d71449efb0aa5c10549752b5d75384d8](https://github.com/cvg/LightGlue/blob/eb42fee2d71449efb0aa5c10549752b5d75384d8/lightglue/lightglue.py#L258) | Code multiplies row/column softmax assignment terms by learned endpoint matchabilities, adds unmatched entries, then applies mutual selection and a score threshold. Original keypoint slots can remain -1 with zero matching score after mapping pruned indices back. Learned rejection and explicit unmatched bookkeeping are existing mechanisms. They do not identify whether a missing match means occlusion, blur, repeated texture, or incorrect camera response. |

A minimal strong baseline is a fixed-anchor denominator plus explicit missing outcomes. Freeze anchor IDs before the intervention; define the eligible overlap using only the declared geometric transform and image bounds. Keep the matching rule fixed. For each target, report N eligible anchors, M accepted correspondences and the accepted residual statistic. An intersection of matched anchor IDs is useful only as a secondary statistic conditional on joint survival.

One scale-free, prospective alternative readout is q_i = 1[e_H(i) < e_I(i)], with ties zero, for an accepted correspondence. Let S be its accepted successes. Assign no-match an unknown value in [0,1], rather than treating it as perfect agreement or camera error. The completion bounds for the full-anchor response fraction are exactly [S/N, (S+N-M)/N]. These are elementary missing-data bounds, not confidence intervals, calibrated camera probabilities, or proof that accepted matches are correct. If N=0 the readout is undefined. This is a possible future readout, not a replacement for S76's frozen median comparison.

For two proposed observers, the improvement interval is [L_new-U_old, U_new-L_old]. If it includes zero, an improved accepted median does not identify improved population response under these assumptions. The strongest cheap competitor is ordinary confidence-only filtering of the same raw candidates at matched accepted count, with its rank/tie rule fixed in advance; no generator change is required. If a proposed uncertainty mechanism offers only the same survivor improvement, its claimed benefit is explained by standard filtering. A strict lower bound above zero would support this limited response comparison, while still requiring correspondence validity and adequate spatial coverage. Poor coverage remains inconclusive rather than evidence of success.

The elementary counterexample is to discard the largest residuals without changing either image: accepted error decreases while the generated camera behavior is identical. Thus “unknown-aware guidance” is not novel merely because it rejects matches. A worthwhile candidate would need to change generated response and beat this fixed-information baseline, rather than just its reporting population.

The previous EquiVDM/Generative Photography boundary remains: common image-grid noise does not by itself guarantee the same physical scene, and a trivial output homography can satisfy an overlap response test without a world model. This batch adds no claim that a response diagnostic establishes innovation, overall image quality, or a unique cause of S70 framing.
