# Inference-time camera correction: nearest mechanisms and next decision

**Decision:** Broad claims of training-free camera correction, geometric feedback during sampling, or adaptive guidance are already occupied. A source-memory allocation question remains worth specifying, but this batch does not establish its novelty or choose a method. S76 results were not read and its protocol remains unchanged.

| Primary work | Control signal and allowed observations | Additional cost and failure boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **WorldForge**, Chenxi Song, Yanming Yang, Tong Zhao, Ruibo Li, Chi Zhang. [arXiv v3, 21 March 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.15130v3), official CVPR 2026 listing/project. | Source image/video → estimated depth/pose → requested-trajectory render and mask. IRR replaces visible clean-latent content and re-noises; FLF selects channels using flow agreement with rendered trajectory latents; DSG corrects using guided/unguided velocity differences and cosine weighting. The paper's “GT” trajectory flow is this warped-source surrogate, not a withheld target photograph. No final-output estimated-pose feedback loop is required by these equations. | Extra denoiser evaluation/refinement during roughly the first 20 of 50 steps, plus geometry, VAE and flow computations. Appendix runtime table reports IRR 14.1 s within a 29.0 s example step on A100; this is not a CPU estimate. Severe depth errors and complex dynamics remain failure cases. §§3.1.2–3.4, appendix runtime/limitations. |

[arXiv v1, 8 December 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06029v1); official ICCV 2025 indexed paper. | Decode a partly denoised prediction, estimate time-aware point clouds and poses using MonST3R, shift/render to the requested relative trajectory, re-encode, then inpaint and harmonize. The official WorldForge repository is pinned at `ee573a051715a451b806a90e21462f23308faac4` (10 April 2026). Its [README](https://github.com/Westlake-AGI-Lab/WorldForge/blob/ee573a051715a451b806a90e21462f23308faac4/README.md#L203) exposes warped frames/masks through `--video-ref`, guidance duration, per-step resampling and DSG strength. It warns that excessive resampling can lose trajectory and excessive guidance can introduce artifacts. The actual inference source retrieval failed TLS; this is interface/paper verification, not a code-equivalence audit. Latent-Reframe's accepted PDF was inaccessible; method/runtime details above deliberately refer to v1, not an assumed unchanged final version.

These are substantive baselines against an unspecified “observe response, correct camera” proposal. They are not evidence that every possible outer-loop pose servo has been solved. Crucially, guided/unguided disagreement is not an external camera measurement: symbolically, if both paths predict the same wrong velocity, their difference is zero. A correct warp constraint can likewise coexist with hallucinated content outside its support. Optimizing only survivor matches can hide that failure, as the preceding selectivity note explains.

**The narrower proposed question:** With a fixed history budget k, the same available observations and a fixed total denoiser-call budget, can a source-selection rule predict which history exchange improves requested-camera response *and* preserves observed scene content, beyond ordinary pose proximity and greedy visible-support coverage? Recompute all descendants of the exchanged source. Keep the corrector and its schedule identical across selection arms; charge any pilot generation or feedback call to both budgets. Target RGB must remain evaluation-only. These two papers do not establish an answer to that specific allocation question; this is not evidence that the wider literature lacks one.

**Simplest strong baseline and rejection:** Use an unchanged corrector with nearest-pose or greedy complementary-visibility selection at the same k and compute; also retain the unchanged-selection arm. If extra correction calls explain the effect, or ordinary coverage selection matches it, reject a source-allocation mechanism claim. A final-output homography is a cheap geometry-only control that can kill a response-only claim, but cannot establish newly revealed scene content. Missing correspondence support remains unknown, never an automatic gain.

After S76, first classify its frozen endpoint: adequate supported response argues against adding correction merely because absolute framing looks wrong; weak/misdirected response justifies considering correction baselines; insufficient support does not authorize optimizing the observer until it agrees. No new run, coefficient choice or model port is authorized by this note. NO_METHOD_SELECTED remains.
