**Potential difference to test, explicitly not a novelty claim.** A consumer could retain a distribution over compatible historical correspondences, including an explicit no-reliable-correspondence state, and let that uncertainty limit the geometric guidance contribution rather than first selecting one match or treating a rendered pixel as certain. The substantive object would be the *guidance field actually applied during denoising*, with ambiguity carried into consumption. This differs conceptually from merely pruning the diagnostic's matches or improving a score by reporting fewer points. However, null/dustbin matching, probabilistic correspondence, robust estimation, visibility masks, and confidence gates are established ideas. Adding one of them to an epipolar block is insufficient novelty. This limited two-paper pass has not established a new estimator, objective, or advantage over those ordinary baselines; further exact-neighbor verification would be necessary before choosing it.

The falsifiable scientific question is narrower than “uncertainty helps”: with identical available history and fixed requested cameras, can ambiguous-support handling reduce *wrong geometric updates* while retaining useful constraint strength on identifiable support? A future comparison must retain every target and report a fixed support/coverage denominator alongside errors. At minimum compare ordinary geometry masking, simple confidence weighting, and the no-guidance branch under the same information and computational budget. Improvement achieved solely by rejecting more pixels or reverting everywhere to the base model would not support the proposed mechanism. This is a proposed future comparison, not an amendment to the running S73 contract or permission to run another model.

**Strongest elementary counterexample.** Consider rectified, calibrated static views of a periodically textured surface. A source patch has two indistinguishable candidate target patches, q0=(u0,v,1) and q1=(u1,v,1), on the same epipolar line. Both satisfy the supplied fundamental-matrix equation exactly, but at most one represents that physical point. A wrong depth estimate can project densely onto q1, giving complete render coverage, and a feature similarity score may confidently choose q1. Neither epipolar residual nor render occupancy reveals the mistake. An explicit unknown state can avoid an unjustified force but cannot recover the missing identity: if the permitted observations are identical under both explanations, every estimator using only those observations must have the same output distribution under both. Selecting the correct identity therefore requires extra distinguishing evidence or an assumption; abstention alone cannot guarantee correct framing. This is symbolic reasoning, not a measured property of the TUM scene or either paper's trained model.

An additional boundary follows from the previous fixed-F audit: translation scale along a common direction can leave F unchanged. Even perfect correspondence uncertainty cannot restore motion information absent from the epipolar constraint. Therefore a successful residual-guidance result would still not certify the complete requested camera or solve S70's root cause.
