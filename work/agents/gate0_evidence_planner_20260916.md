## Minimal order of operations

1. Fill and independently review `CAMERA_CONTRACT.json`, `DEPTH_CONTRACT.json`, `PAIRING_CONTRACT.json`, and `POSE_CONTRACT.json` from read-only evidence.
2. Freeze history/calibration/future windows and selector/scorer isolation in `FREEZE.json`; do not read test-future bytes before the hash is recorded.
3. Acquire and hash one independent held-out source under the frozen protocol; mark any pre-exposed candidate as development-only.
4. Create `S103_PRE_RUN_MANIFEST.json`, bind source/checkpoint/config hashes, and run `python3 work/S102_gate0_tum/validate_gate0_contract.py` locally (or the pinned environment's `bin/python` remotely).
5. Only a new signed contract revision with every section `PASS` may submit S103. Use `work/S102_gate0_tum/REMOTE_GPU_LAUNCH_PATTERN_20260916.md`: submit from persistent `tmux`/`screen`, never a foreground SSH process.

## Why S103 is not yet startable

The TUM qualification receipt proves data availability and timestamped pairing only. It does not prove camera calibration binding, pose semantics, future-answer isolation, independent held-out identity, or a fixed fair budget. Therefore the first formal selector-free VMem baseline must wait for the artifacts above; model-load and component smoke tests may continue separately.
