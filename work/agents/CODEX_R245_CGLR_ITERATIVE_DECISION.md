# R245 CGLR iterative decision

## Decision

**Retire CGLR as an actionable benchmark contribution at the current evidence state (END-LINE).** The narrow conjunction remains a logically expressible research question, but R244 makes clear that identification requires an owner packet, sealed outcomes, independent audits, and executed paired evidence that do not exist. No promotion or novelty claim is authorized.

## Comparison with closest overlaps

- **Abstention/selective prediction:** AbstentionBench and conformal LLM/VLM work already measure unsupported-answer rejection and risk/coverage.
- **Frame ambiguity:** COMFORT, FoREST, and ViewSpatial-Bench already manipulate/evaluate camera/person/reference frames.
- **Source attribution:** MAVIS evaluates multimodal citations and groundedness.
- **Active evidence acquisition:** BCEA and SIEVES evaluate acquire/re-examine or visual evidence scoring.

The surviving conjunction (same visible artifact, provenance-only reveal, hidden counter-world, false-unique acceptance) is narrower in mechanism, but no measured evidence shows it adds information beyond these overlaps.

## Supporting evidence

- R242/R243 found no source explicitly combining all four conjunction elements.
- R244 supplies a falsifiable 2×2 design with controls and leakage thresholds.

## Contradictory evidence / blockers

- Existing overlaps can explain any abstention gain without provenance-specific reasoning.
- R244's required owner packet, sealed outcomes, canonical diff, side-channel audit, adjudication, and held-out observations are all missing.
- A protocol schema alone cannot establish identifiability or benchmark value.

## Falsifiable prediction

If provenance-only reveal is genuinely distinct, its difference-in-differences effect on false-unique acceptance will exceed the preregistered margin over provenance-neutral, frame, source, and acquisition controls while preserving unique-control acceptance. Failure of this condition retires the distinction.

## Strongest competing explanation

Any structured intervention that adds context, caution, frame wording, citations, or extra visual evidence triggers generic abstention; provenance itself contributes no unique causal information.

## Information-gain rationale

The next measurement would only be valuable if it can discriminate H1 from this generic-intervention explanation under R244 controls. Without sealed counter-worlds and independent side-channel/diff audits, another benchmark result would add no trustworthy information and risks re-measuring abstention.

## Final status

Retire the current CGLR benchmark proposal as `END-LINE`; keep only a documented falsifiable question for possible future owner-supplied evidence. Remain `B_STATIC_ONLY`; no parser, data/model access, benchmark, evaluation, GPU/Slurm, receipt, or flag action occurred.
