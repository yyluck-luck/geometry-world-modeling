## Closest mechanisms and evaluation boundaries

* **4Real-Video, CVPR 2025.** §3 constructs a time×view grid and exchanges information between temporal and view streams. It conditions on an entire fixed-view video and a freeze-time view sequence; the supplied motion can itself be generated. Thus it addresses multiview completion of a specified motion realization, not necessarily past-only forecasting. §4.2 uses generated testing inputs without paired 4D ground truth, reports video-distribution/quality metrics separately, and measures GIM/DUSt3R confidence on freeze-time slices with fixed trajectories. These are learned proxies, not proof of geometry. Its hard-synchronization discussion reports stretching and unintended motion during nominally frozen time. [Official CVPR record](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/virtual/2025/poster/34309); [read author v1, §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.04462v1).
* **MEt3R, CVPR 2025.** §3 explicitly targets consistency independently of matching a particular true scene. DUSt3R pointmaps warp DINO/FeatUp features between generated images; bidirectional cosine similarity defines the score. It needs neither supplied GT depth/pose nor paired future RGB. §5 studies static-scene NVS and video outputs and separates consistency from fidelity. Its reconstruction and features remain learned estimators; a pose-free score cannot certify compliance with requested camera poses. Applying it to different times of a moving object additionally breaks the same-scene premise unless motion is accounted for. [Official CVPR record](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Asim_MET3R_Measuring_Multi-View_Consistency_in_Generated_Images_CVPR_2025_paper.html); [read author v1, §§3,5](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.06336v1).
* **4DiM, ICLR 2025, reused and rechecked.** §4 separates image/video fidelity, epipolar consistency, recovered-pose agreement, reconstruction metrics and motion. TSED uses requested camera geometry, while SfM estimates poses; reference-image scores are not generic sample-quality measures. Its §5.2 motion scores are not proof of the true hidden trajectory. [Read §§4–5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.07860v2); [formal publication](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/ef62614753535977071395fb1f1435be-Abstract-Conference.html). BulletTime's video re-filming and WorldStereo's geometric conditioning remain nearby mechanisms; neither makes an arbitrary unknown future RGB unique. Their earlier read scopes are reused without additional downloads.

## What “the same sampled world” must mean

Write an intended joint sample as {I(v,t)} drawn once for several camera indices v and physical times t. All views at a particular t purport to show the same realized object states and actions. This can be implemented by a joint multiview diffusion draw or rendering a single sampled scene/trajectory representation. It is a **contract to test**, not a guarantee that the model satisfies it. Reusing the same seed in separate camera-conditioned calls does not establish a common latent physical world. Nor are consecutive frames along a moving camera automatically simultaneous views.

At a synchronized instant, corresponding visible material points must satisfy the known-camera epipolar relation and admit a common positive-depth 3D location. The object can be moving or deforming: stationarity across time is unnecessary for this instantaneous test. Distinctive object identities can also be checked where visibility supports correspondence; different visible counts across views are not automatically contradictions because of occlusion.

Several tempting substitutes require extra assumptions. A single homography generally assumes a plane or pure camera rotation; cross-time static triangulation fails on moving points. RGB equality after warping assumes favorable illumination/material behavior, particularly doubtful for reflective or transparent objects. Warping with GT depth from the *realized* future tests agreement with that realization, not self-consistency of a different sampled future. A fitted neural reconstruction is not a 3D oracle. Low epipolar error alone can hide mismatches outside its sparse support; a completely static imagined scene can be self-consistent yet fail the dynamics task.

## One residual falsifiable question and the cheapest diagnostic

**Question:** for an actual jointly sampled same-time output, can the claimed moving object's correspondences reject a common 3D configuration under the requested cameras while static-background correspondences remain compatible? This is a conventional geometric failure witness, not a new metric or demonstrated memory defect. It separates an internal contradiction from merely choosing a different plausible future; it does not uniquely separate foreground distortion from local camera noncompliance or establish a memory cause.

The smallest useful CPU route is a fixed synchronized three-camera triplet with adequate overlap/parallax. First validate calibration and annotation tolerances on one real simultaneous triplet from the existing recording. Then, only if a separately authorized **joint** generated triplet exists, apply the same declared rule: annotate a small set of unambiguous material landmarks, triangulate from two views, and evaluate reprojection in the third without refitting it. Report all attempted foreground/background points, visibility, residuals and missing matches. No dense network or ground-truth depth is necessary. The real triplet is an instrument check, not the unique target appearance for the generated one. No such computation or pixel inspection occurred here.

Stop if timestamps are only frame-slot guesses, independent samples were mixed, calibration fails its real-data control, parallax is degenerate, or correspondences are ambiguous. Preserve UNKNOWN instead of converting absence of matches into a geometric failure. If the joint output passes, that case provides no contradiction; it still does not demonstrate correct future actions, rich dynamics or useful memory. If only ordinary independent-view generation fails and an existing joint-sampling baseline fixes it, the remedy is established prior art. No new model/arm, normalization scheme, uncertainty gate or claim of innovation follows from this review.
