## Important limitations already established by sources

Neural3D cameras are estimated by COLMAP and held fixed; the GoPro linear mode motivates their pinhole approximation. Timecode synchronization and removal of inconsistent streams support matched times, but do not specify a numerical residual synchronization tolerance in the inspected document. Actual poses/K conventions, video PTS, missing frames and compression are not checked by a successful HEAD. The public license is CC-BY-NC4.0, per the official README. Do not substitute a rendered novel view, slow-motion interpolation or model latent extrapolation for the observed heldout movie. [Official README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/Neural_3D_Video), [supplement §2](https://neural-3d-video.github.io/resources/supp.pdf).

DyCheck's guide uses audio-derived integer frame offsets and excludes testing frames blocked by the training camera. Its actual preprocessing source notes training RGB/Lidar timing mismatch: it uses RGB from point-cloud capture, reports a small empirical validation synchronization residual (<10ms), and copies capture fps into extra metadata. This is a documented approximation, not exact simultaneity at arbitrary physical contact. [Capture guide](https://github.com/KAIR-BAIR/dycheck/blob/main/docs/RECORD3D_CAPTURE.md), [official source lines295–299](https://github.com/KAIR-BAIR/dycheck/blob/main/dycheck/datasets/iphone.py#L295).

DyCheck co-visibility masks summarize visibility across the original full training video and require a minimum number of training observations; they cannot silently become past-prefix eligibility when that prefix excludes later frames. Keypoint annotations cover10 uniformly sampled training frames and contain position/visibility, not free dense object-centroid truth. Metric iPhone depth is provided only for training frames. Keep these as separately declared evaluation evidence; do not leak labels or future frames into a selector. [Dataset schema](https://github.com/KAIR-BAIR/dycheck/blob/main/docs/DATASETS.md).
