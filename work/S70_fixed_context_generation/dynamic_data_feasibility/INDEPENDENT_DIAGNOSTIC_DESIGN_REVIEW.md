# Independent review: the smallest dynamic-history diagnostic

Reviewer: `/root/c2_v9_source_primary`; different from the data-metadata author. Recorded 2026-09-09, with exact final timestamp and input hashes in the accompanying receipt. No video/image/depth/NPY/model-weight body was read, no data was downloaded, and the running S70 experiment was untouched.

**Decision: a conditional, annotated image-plane motion pilot is feasible in principle; a heldout-view prediction or state-sufficiency experiment is not yet certified. No new selector mechanism is justified.** The current work establishes a reachable synchronized-camera dataset and usable pose metadata, not a visible dynamic interval or a predictive failure.

## What the actual metadata establishes

The saved official [Neural 3D Video README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/Neural_3D_Video) defines cam00 as the heldout reference and maps poses to sorted existing streams. Independent arithmetic on the final `CAMERAS.json` confirms all 18 reported axis conversions and center distances: cam06 is row 5, nearest to cam00 at 1.0730474992699424 reconstruction units; cam05 follows at 1.088017850776411. These are not certified meters. This review read the final JSON, not the original pose NPY, and did not repeat pose acquisition.

The metadata author's successful receipt records 2,246 downloaded archive bytes for calibration and proposes two MP4 members totaling 130,923,253 compressed bytes. No media was decoded in that work. Actual video dimensions, PTS alignment, residual synchronization error, object identity, occlusion and usable motion remain unverified. The declared centered/equal-focal K and nearby centers do not certify visibility or pixel-accurate cross-view correspondence. The earlier feasibility note's proposed pose retrieval is now historical; the final pose receipt records its actual completion.

Two cameras observing the same event supply two views of **one realized future**, not two independent futures. They are not independent trajectories or repeated trials.

## Two design problems to resolve before a prediction claim

1. **Fixed cam06 makes pose-only temporal selection degenerate.** Its historical camera pose/FoV is constant, so pose-only scores tie across times. An old-frame tie break losing to recent frames is an ordinary temporal-baseline failure, not evidence for a complementary-pair mechanism. A scene-dependent visibility score is a different selector and would require extra measured scene information.
2. **A cam06 2D track does not specify a cam00 2D track.** Camera K and poses leave depth unresolved: each cam06 point identifies a ray, not a unique world point. A primary future-cam00 location/appearance score therefore needs a declared cross-view predictor plus suitable past depth/correspondences or a justified planar model. Supplying cam00 past annotations to triangulate would make cam00 an additional input camera and change its heldout role. No such geometry is supplied by these metadata alone.

The cheapest proposal below evaluates motion in cam06's own image plane. Cam00 stays out of the predictor and can provide separately annotated synchronized visibility/identity corroboration after sealing predictions. This is explicitly narrower than predicting the heldout camera. If heldout-cam00 prediction is mandatory, stop at the missing cross-view contract rather than silently claim this pilot fulfills it.

## A concrete small pilot, for a later frozen protocol

Use one prefix, one tracked material landmark on a clearly identifiable moving rigid object, one query horizon and a common elementary predictor. Avoid a liquid centroid or silhouette center: deformation and visibility can move those without measuring a persistent physical point. Object/point choice uses only the permitted cam06 prefix and is recorded before future access. If a reliable point is absent, record infeasibility; do not search the future for a more persuasive event.

A concrete starting rule is cutoff 2.0 seconds after the first verified common video time, history interval [1.0, 2.0] seconds and query 0.2 seconds after the cutoff. These are prospective choices, not verified timestamps. Resolve them against actual PTS under a fixed nearest-frame/tie rule before decoding the prefix. Inspect only that prefix to establish point identity and past visibility; do not choose the cutoff/horizon by examining later contact, acceleration or error. If the fixed interval is unusable, retain that result rather than quietly move it. A later revision would be a separately labeled design.

Retain **k=2** past measurements for each equal-budget probe: the latest two eligible observations; two uniformly spaced interval endpoints; and a conventional query-specific linear information-design pair. For the last rule, enumerate the available time pairs and minimize `c_q^T (A_S^T A_S)^(-1) c_q`, with rows `[1, t_i−t_cut]` and `c_q=[1, 0.2]`, under an explicitly provisional equal-independent localization-noise model. Ties use timestamp order. This is ordinary linear design, not a proposed new algorithm. Fit the same straight-line image-coordinate predictor in every arm. The all-past fit is a larger-input diagnostic reference, not an equal-budget competitor. A pose-only deterministic tie arm, if retained, is a sanity check with its tie rule disclosed.

Manually annotated past point positions/visibility are extra information. Supply the same annotation procedure and eligible candidate pool to all probe arms; the predictor receives only each arm's selected measurements and timestamps. Since prefix inspection/annotation may access all candidate images, **k=2 is retained predictor-input budget, not total raw-image access budget or an end-to-end image selector**. Count annotation/access cost. Freeze selected IDs, predictions, common predictor and scoring rule before opening either future stream. This design must not be described as testing actual VMem selection or generation.

After freezing, annotate the prescribed future time in cam06 and cam00 without showing the annotator arm predictions. Report cam06 Euclidean point error in decoded-image pixels, annotation uncertainty and visibility at the fixed horizon. If the point is hidden, mark the geometric endpoint unscorable; retain its missingness and cam00 corroboration, and do not replace it with a more visible time or object. A cam00 annotation cannot retroactively supply cam06 depth or alter the query. The one query is one exploratory case, not a distributional accuracy estimate; pixels, cameras and overlapping time windows do not multiply independent sample size.

## Time, visibility and velocity boundaries

- Full MP4 acquisition necessarily reads compressed bytes containing future content. Record those bytes honestly. Seal future semantic access: no future display, annotation, tracking, motion-based synchronization tuning, parameter selection or masks until predictions are fixed. Stream/PTS metadata may be inspected first under the declared rule. Decoder dependencies may decode later reference frames internally; this must not be misreported as zero future bytes or automatically permit exposing those frames to the predictor.
- Sync claims support investigating matched times; they do not establish exact simultaneity. Use PTS and documented offsets, with an explicit timing uncertainty. Any extra alignment derived from motion must use only the allowed prefix. No unverified millisecond tolerance is assumed here.
- Past visibility eligibility may use only past observations. Future visibility is evaluation information. Annotation over a complete movie, future-derived co-visibility masks or retrospectively smoothed tracks cannot silently enter the prefix. Do not filter a window for being contact-free by inspecting its future; an unanticipated contact is a possible failure of the predictor, not an exclusion invented afterward.
- The fitted derivative is **2D projected velocity in pixels/second**. Even with a fixed camera, perspective depth changes, rotation, occlusion and localization error matter. Metric 3D velocity requires an additional scale/depth/correspondence contract. A second view can help measurement if used appropriately, but does not provide that contract automatically.

## Stop rules and the claim this can support

If recent or uniform time preserves useful motion, use that ordinary baseline. If the conventional information-design pair explains the difference, this is known-design integration. If full history does not help, there is no observed history-budget problem for this probe. If full history helps, larger sample count/noise averaging, predictor misspecification and annotation noise remain competing explanations; a weak selected-history predictor is not proof that the selected images lack the information.

One fixed-camera trajectory cannot prove that identical observations admit different future physical states, estimate conditional entropy, show irreducible Bayes error, or isolate selector loss from the decoder. Such claims need a separate identification argument or genuinely matched repeated trajectories/interventions; repeated-looking poses or frames are insufficient. The present recommendation is to stop generic pair-mechanism invention and, if root proceeds, obtain this one bounded real-data diagnostic first. It supplies a local falsification opportunity, not novelty evidence or authorization to expand the running experiment.
