# Fixed-budget memory: predictive state is the question, not a new mechanism yet

UTC: 2026-09-09T03:12:51.344479+00:00. Author: `/root/negative_result_question_triage`.
**Decision: KEEP_ONE_REAL_DATA_DIAGNOSTIC; NO_QUALIFIED_NEW_MECHANISM_YET.** Three new primary methods/theory read, plus targeted reuse. No S70 outputs consulted or frozen conditions changed.

The broad proposal “preserve velocity/state changes rather than only spatial coverage” has substantial prior art. This does not prove that every concrete finite-budget selector is dominated, or that further work is impossible. The unverified issue is narrower: which useful distinction is lost by this actual selector, under this actual input and cost contract, and whether an ordinary equal-budget alternative already fixes it.

Memory Forcing's already-read §3.3/Eq3 mixes temporal and spatial retrieval by exploration/revisit regime; it remains an ordinary baseline collision. ReMind already appears in the S43 consumption/training audit. No fresh method claims about it were taken from this round's search snippets.

**A precise counterexample, and its limitation.** Consider a stylized point whose visible positions at common times 0,1,2 are `(0,+1,0)` in history A and `(0,−1,0)` in B. It reverses direction at time1 and then continues at constant velocity. At time3 the positions are respectively −1 and +1. A selector retaining only times0 and2 sees the same point images, cameras and the same retained timestamps, yet discards the intermediate direction evidence. Recent times1 and2 distinguish the cases with the same two-frame budget. This is a hand-defined aliasing example, not a real image/model result, not an observed choice of the VMem selector, and not proof that all spatial policies fail. Indeed, a spatial policy that retains times1 and2 succeeds. Even broad uniform temporal coverage can alias across a state change; “more spread in time” is not a universal cure.

Under a *valid constant-velocity model* with position observations, rows `[1,t_i]` form the observation matrix. Two distinct times have determinant `t_2−t_1`, so rank is full even from a fixed camera with calibrated visible point coordinates. Choosing timestamps to improve the information matrix or the variance of a particular future prediction is ordinary model-based experimental design. Rank alone says little about unmodelled contact switches, appearance ambiguity or moving-camera image velocity. Do not infer rank deficiency from the label “geometry driven.”

**One falsifiable candidate measurement.** For one independently timestamped real dynamic track, with a future observation kept out of selection/fitting, compare the actual spatial selector with **the same four-frame budget** of recent frames and uniform-time frames. Use the same observation extractor, available times/cameras, ordering convention and a forecast rule fixed from a separate visible control segment. An all-history estimator is only an information-headroom diagnostic, not an equal-cost method. State annotations are extra information unless equally available to every compared estimator; a centroid proxy is not a claim about RGB generation or 3D contact state.

The useful next decision is whether either ordinary four-frame baseline recovers the forecast signal lost by the spatial subset. If it does, this witness does **not** justify a complex new policy. If none differs, record that the chosen probe/witness did not support loss; never infer complete information preservation or decoder-only blame. If full history itself fails the visible control, stop the compression attribution. A harder mechanism question needs a verified case where simple alternatives also fail but useful state information remains accessible under the actual budget. The present sources do not establish that conjunction for this VMem consumer. Data feasibility is assigned to another agent; this report performs no duplicate search of scientific payloads.

**Independent check of Gemini.** The root's conditional-mean risk identity and symmetric-variance counterexample are correct under their stated assumptions. Equal MSE, even at ideal Bayes risk, does not imply distributional sufficiency. Positive conditional mutual information need not mean deterministic physical evolution, and deterministic chaos is not synonymous with stochastic noise. Different retained timestamps are different inputs when actually consumed. See the companion mathematical check for the exact scope and bound root SHA.

No new algorithm is selected. Differences in input access, query dependence, state parameterization or cost could support a future concrete contribution, but those differences alone are not validated benefit or novelty. The next valuable evidence is the small equal-budget real-data comparison, not another generic gate or literature list.
