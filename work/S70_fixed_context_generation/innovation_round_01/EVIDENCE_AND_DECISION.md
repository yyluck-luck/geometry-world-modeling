## The hidden assumption to reject

Changing A=`[19,18,13,12]` to B=`[19,18,14,13]` changes real reference information, camera slots, the CLIP mean, latent placement, and the naturally computed camera convention. In the accepted S69 report the scalars are 27.322040557861328 and 26.265939712524414. A generator **may appropriately change** when given different evidence. Therefore output sensitivity alone is neither a defect nor a benefit. Freezing a conventional training-time normalization could even hurt. The strongest ordinary explanation is useful or conflicting reference content and camera arrangement, with scale merely accompanying it.

The bound original `pipeline.py:1089–1195` constructs a robust camera center and uses `s = camera_scale / ||centered first camera|| + 0.01` outside its near-zero branch. Its ray moment changes with s; directions do not in exact arithmetic. `get_cond` has no depth or latent-warp input. Its outputs include the changed cameras as well as c/uc. `sampling.py:194–221` also has a translation-distance threshold in the original MultiviewCFG rule. Any later scalar intervention must recompute all these descendants; it must not assume that editing one ray tensor isolates the complete path.

Three operations must remain distinct: (i) changing the world coordinate frame, (ii) changing the unit of the same physical reconstruction, and (iii) changing which reference observations are supplied. Only the first two can be coordinate symmetries under an appropriately specified simultaneous transformation. Replacing source12 with source14 is not a group action on a fixed observation bundle. Nor should an encoder discard meaningful physical translation just to make its numbers invariant.

A source-level caveat, not a new experiment: with fixed valid membership, nonzero first-camera norm d, and raw centers multiplied by u>0, the formula gives `(camera_scale/(u*d)+0.01)*u*t = camera_scale*t/d + 0.01*u*t`. Thus its additive term is not exactly unit invariant. Validity clipping and the near-zero branch also need the stated domain. This elementary fact neither quantifies an output problem nor makes removing the constant novel.
