# Batch 06 decision: an information witness must be asymmetric

Actual UTC: 2026-09-09T03:27:54Z → 2026-09-09T03:31:20.744505+00:00. Author: `/root/negative_result_question_triage`. **NO_METHOD_SELECTED.** Two primary papers were read; no S70 scientific inputs/results, model, dataset or video was accessed.

**Decision.** The cheapest useful next step is a prefix-only eligibility check for one real dynamic pair, followed by a frozen elementary prediction if eligible. A failed generator or probe cannot establish that the history lacks information. Conversely, a successful predictor using exactly the allowed history can demonstrate accessible predictive signal without proving that the generator has encoded or used it.

**Closest concrete design.** *Physion++*, formal NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks, §3.1–3.2, makes simulated trials with matching prediction-start images but different hidden physical properties and outcomes. Earlier interactions provide property clues; some scenes are rearranged behind a curtain. Its comparison with/without those earlier interactions is directly relevant. The full histories differ: it does not establish identical complete model inputs. It is neither real multicamera footage nor a fixed-four-frame memory study. [Official main paper, pp. 5–7](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/d3e8011c912e651ab2a76e7935a1e464-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf)

Do not transfer its entire evaluation recipe: the fully observed diagnostic includes future answers, and the particle model can receive ground-truth properties. The supplement reports selecting the best testing epoch; it also specifies an MLP readout where the main text says logistic regression. These are limits on direct replication and fair attribution, not grounds to dismiss the paired-stimulus idea. Its dataset pairing is outcome-curated, not a prefix-only natural sample. [Official supplement, §§C–E, pp. 3–4](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/d3e8011c912e651ab2a76e7935a1e464-Supplemental-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf)

**Closest mathematical distinction.** Xu et al., *A Theory of Usable Information under Computational Constraints*, ICLR 2020, defines predictive information relative to a specified family of predictors that may ignore inputs. Better preprocessing can improve that family's predictions without creating Shannon information. Its estimation theorem requires a controlled function class and bounded log likelihood; a trained weak probe is not the optimization infimum. Therefore equal probe losses do not establish equal information, and unsuccessful fitting does not prove irreducible uncertainty. [Author manuscript, §§2–4, Definitions 1–3 and Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.10689); [official ICLR listing](https://iclr.cc/virtual_2020/papers.html?filter=authors&search=Stefano+Ermon).

**One qualified real-pair construction, still unexecuted.** Let U be the complete actually consumed ordered k-frame bundle, cameras, timing representation, controls and target query. Match two clips on a declared *current-view* state and query, not on all of U: for example, the same object is near the same image position while earlier allowed observations show leftward versus rightward motion. Their histories are deliberately different. In real footage, appearance matching is approximate and may retain nuisance differences; it cannot prove an exact input collision. A separate synchronized camera can verify object identity, occlusion and later reappearance, but information visible only to that camera is privileged evidence, not information available in U.

The minimal next step is to use the other agent's feasible existing recording, if any, and freeze a time cutoff, k, query horizon, prefix matching rule and elementary motion rule. Establish the cue using only the allowed pre-cutoff views and timestamps; no target camera image, future tracking/smoothing, outcome-based frame selection or test-label tuning enters prediction. Seal predictions before checking the later independent view. Keep all prefix-eligible cases, including equal outcomes and failures; if outcomes were already inspected to choose the pair, label it a curated exploratory witness. A single pair is an existence diagnostic, not generalization or a method comparison. Full-history or extra-view predictors, if considered later, are information-advantaged diagnostics and must be labelled so.

| Observed finding in a later valid diagnostic | Permitted decision |
|---|---|
| A predeclared rule from U predicts the withheld outcomes; the generator fails on the same cases | Evidence of available signal and a model/task mismatch; not proof that the defect is specifically memory encoding, attention or decoding. |
| Only extra views, extra history or future annotations resolve the state | The available-information premise is unestablished; do not attribute the error to failure to use U. |
| Both an elementary rule and the generator fail | Unresolved: observation noise, hidden forces, wrong motion assumptions, probe limits and training/domain mismatch remain alternatives. |

**Stop conditions.** Stop this direction if no verified real pair has an observable prefix cue and independent outcome, or if calibration/timing/identity uncertainty dominates it. If recent/uniform selection already retains the cue, use that ordinary baseline; do not invent a selector. Exact equality of complete U with different required deterministic answers would prove a task ambiguity, but similar current images do not. For a stochastic future, a mixture forecast may be correct. Existing observability, predictive-state and information-design principles remain prior art; no new information-bottleneck mechanism or generation arm is proposed.
