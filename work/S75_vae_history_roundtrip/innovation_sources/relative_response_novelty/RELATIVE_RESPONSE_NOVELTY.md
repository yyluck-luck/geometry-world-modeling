## Crucial distinction for the proposed pilot

Fixing actual image-grid RNG is a valid way to estimate **the implemented generator's response** to changing its camera input. It does not make that random tensor a camera-independent world state. EquiVDM's ideal relation also transports noise, so its theorem does not imply exact homography equivariance for unmodified S70 with identical image-grid noise. A transported-noise experiment would be a separately declared intervention. Ordinary image interpolation also changes Gaussian statistics; it must not be silently substituted into sampling.

A symbolic example makes the distinction precise (my inference, not a paper claim). Let u be isotropic Gaussian, Q(P) a camera-dependent orthogonal transform, and R(P) a correct camera renderer. Both F(P,u)=R(P)u and F'(P,u)=R(P)Q(P)u have the same conditional image distribution at each P. But holding u fixed can change the sampled world in F' when P changes. Therefore a failed same-u cross-camera relation need not uniquely demonstrate incorrect conditional camera marginals. With partial history, this concerns unresolved content; it does not excuse changing already constrained structure.

## Specific falsifiable signature, with an explicit competing explanation

For the already proposed same-center yaw, compute the optical-camera prediction H=K R_newᵀ R_old K⁻¹. On each of the four targets, use exactly the same accepted correspondences for H and identity, keep match availability and spatial coverage, and retain all four targets. The previously proposed all-four positive median[e_identity−e_H] event tests a directional response. A nonpositive target falsifies that event; missing support makes it UNKNOWN. Do not upgrade this sign test to accurate angle, stable scene identity, or physical camera calibration. A global appearance change may preserve feature locations near identity; content replacement or ambiguous matches can also produce failure. These remain competing explanations.

A potential *method* claiming to repair camera/appearance disentanglement would need a stronger signature: reduced **absolute fixed-H residual** at the predeclared amplitude without gaining merely by eliminating hard matches, and preserved scene content where history constrains it. It must beat simple camera-conditioning and noise-transport baselines under their declared information/compute budgets. The strongest immediate counterexample is outputting H-warped A0: it can satisfy the overlap geometry almost perfectly without a world model, reasoning about unseen regions, or producing correct new scene content. Thus passing the yaw diagnostic alone cannot support method novelty. EquiVDM already occupies consistent-noise/geometry alignment, and Generative Photography already occupies differential camera-effect learning/evaluation. No distinct mechanism surviving those baselines was established in this batch.

The cheap pilot can decide whether this declared consumer shows useful relative response and whether a camera-ignored explanation remains tenable. A method decision needs evidence beyond that; it is not licensed by renaming fixed-H matching or a quality gate. Current S75 work and all existing frozen protocols are unchanged.
